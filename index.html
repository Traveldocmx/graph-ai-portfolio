Meet your instructor
Practical, responsible GenAI workflows for clinicians and academics—built around evidence, transparency, and real-world constraints.

Dr. Rodrigo Ville

Dr. Rodrigo Ville is an infectious diseases physician with experience supporting public health programs and responding to acute health emergencies. His work sits at the intersection of clinical decision-making, outbreak operations, and applied epidemiology.

In this portfolio, he demonstrates how LLMs can be used responsibly to accelerate research and professional workflows—without compromising rigor, privacy, or scientific integrity.

  • Outbreak work across dengue, malaria, mpox, rabies, viral hepatitis, and COVID-19 response operations.
  • Technical advisory support for surveillance, preparedness, and risk analysis in public health settings.
  • High-stakes communication: clinical summaries, guideline-style writing, and stakeholder-ready outputs.
Clinical infectious diseases Outbreak preparedness Applied epidemiology Evidence synthesis Scientific communication

How he helps clients

Dr. Rodrigo works with private healthcare professionals and academic individuals to turn complex questions into clear, defensible outputs—documents, analyses, and workflows that can be audited and reused.

  • Convert messy inputs into structured notes, protocols, and report-ready narratives.
  • Build repeatable AI-assisted pipelines for literature screening, extraction, and synthesis.
  • Create clean figures and stakeholder-facing materials with transparent assumptions and limitations.
About the Course
An 8-week live cohort program focused on no-code AI tools for responsible task automation, analysis, and communication.

What it trains

Practical, ethically grounded use of LLMs to create structured outputs, accelerate research workflows, and build shareable assets.

  • Weekly live workshops, plus weekly help sessions and community support.
  • Personalized feedback on assessments and capstone project coaching.
  • Shareable certificate and lifetime access to tutorial materials.

Assessment

The grading rubric combines quizzes, workshop assignments, participation, and a final project. (Target: consistent weekly submissions and a high-quality, responsible capstone.)

Quizzes: 15% Assignments: 35% Participation: 10% Final project: 40%
